# Platform extension — 19 November 2026

The user approved a single implementation pass, replacing the pasted per-stage confirmation gates. Existing Next.js/React/Tailwind, Neon Auth/Postgres and Vercel remain in use. Interactive-state visibility is repaired before new modules; UI-STATE-AUDIT.md records the browser permission limit.

New slices: research drafts and source evidence; guide profiles, invitations, calendars and assignments; checkout/discount/refund/transfer ledgers; booking-scoped rich chat, private image assets and safety events; assistant, bots, waitlists/deals, loyalty and owner digest. Existing public shares continue excluding private data. Existing cameras, weather, plan and booking APIs remain available.

Money uses integer minor units and settlement currency from the accepted quote. The browser never supplies a payable amount. Coupons/vouchers reserve and redeem atomically; refunds and completion transfers use durable IDs and provider idempotency. Guide self-reported completion does not release funds without manager approval, actual paid funds and eligible Connect capability.

Independent licensed research fields can become durable drafts. Google content is displayed live, with provider attribution, and is not copied into a permanent OSM catalog or sent to an LLM. Official price extraction is bounded to permitted public hosts with DNS/private-address checks and recorded permission; every candidate remains an estimate until owner confirmation.

Guide access requires a verified role plus an accepted assignment. Client guide contacts are revealed only after payment/fully funded voucher confirmation. In-site chat begins after booking confirmation; prepayment messages cannot exchange contact details. Private photos are re-encoded without EXIF, size-limited and authorised on every read. Offline packs are explicitly downloaded by the client; the service worker does not broadly cache authenticated APIs.

Migrations are tested on an isolated Neon branch before production. Provider-backed paths remain configuration-required until real credentials, account eligibility and sender/source permissions are available. Test records do not send real messages or move real funds.
